## The theme

**1.1 opened the funnel. 1.2 is about the people who walk through it.** Each item removes a reason a
real user gives up: the upload wall before they can see a single job, ghost postings that waste an
afternoon, and a write-up that argues whether you fit rather than telling you what to change.

Confirmed at close, with one honest caveat: **item 7 does not belong to the theme.** It is a seed
employer add that happened to land inside the span. The theme holds for six of seven.

The through-line that only became visible at close: **three of the six were the same class of bug —
something true was being computed and then not delivered.** The window filtered a posting whose match
arrived late (D-108); a link check that could not complete was recorded as a dead job (D-110); a
verdict was computed and the reason for it left implicit (D-111). None was a fetch failure or a model
failure. Every one was a delivery failure downstream of correct work, which is the failure mode a
pipeline with good internal gates is most likely to hide from itself.

## Measured before/after

**Delivery — the quarantine fix (item 5, D-110).** The before is the 08-06 measurement that
justified the PR; the after is the first send with the fix live *and* the extraction outage cleared
(2026-08-22 recovery send).

| | Before (08-06) | After (08-22) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Matched pairs held out of every future digest | **564 of 2,703** (1 in 5) | — |
| HTTP requests per send | **2,727** for 462 distinct URLs (5.9×) | **60** URLs checked, 170 repeats served from cache |
| Quarantined in the send | 125 pairs behind 113 URLs | **11** across 36 recipients |
| Dead links actually found | 1 of 12 hand-rechecked | **1** of 60 |

The 5.9× request amplification is gone (one check per URL per Job, 250ms per-host spacing), and
quarantine stopped being a synonym for "we could not tell": 11 held out of 219 delivered.

**Display — the age floor (item 4, D-109).** Chosen against the production distribution: 897 open
in-scope rows, of which a 21-day floor cut 349 (**39%**). Measured again on 08-22: **912 open
in-scope, 492 inside the floor** — the floor is now removing 46% of the in-scope set, so the corpus
does skew older than it did when the number was picked, and the toggle is doing more work than it
was sized for.

**Cost, unplanned.** D-109's reasoning was extended to extraction on 08-22 (D-112) and the number
justifies the extension retroactively: of the 15,899 untouched rows, **8,715 (55%) were already past
the floor** — work the pipeline would have paid for and could never have displayed.

**Not measured, and it should have been.** Items 2 (paste résumé) and 6 (actionable advice) shipped
without a before/after, because neither has an instrumented outcome — there is no funnel metric on
paste-vs-upload and no quality metric on the write-up. D-111's manual quality signoff is still owed
(see *Open human items*) and would have been item 6's number.

## Known and not fixed

**New in 1.2, and the most expensive one is a measurement gap rather than a bug:**

- **Nothing alerts on silence.** A digest that fails to send is an alert (D-037); a digest that sends
  *nothing* is not, and a pipeline that extracts nothing is not either. Both were true for four days
  starting 08-18 and neither raised anything. This is the single item most responsible for that
  outage lasting as long as it did, and it needs real design — today's permanent Stage-A residue
  means "backlog is non-empty" is always true, so the naive predicate would page every night.
- **`/demo` looks fresh when it is stale.** The activity column keys on `COALESCE(source_updated_at,
  first_seen_at)`, and a reopen (D-053) resets `first_seen_at` while keeping an old `extracted_at`,
  so re-dated and reopened rows sit at the top looking new. Recorded here as cosmetic; the outage
  made it consequential, because the public board kept looking healthy while the product had stopped
  delivering.
- **The dashboard orders on the ATS date; the digest ships on our detection date.** A re-stamped role
  returns to the top of the table looking new when it was mailed days ago. Unchanged from the
  original diagnosis below — it needs a presentation call, not a data fix.
- **`pipeline_runs` records no run duration** — `finished_at` equals `started_at` to the microsecond
  on all 84 runs, so the alert-window margins cannot be checked against real run times.
- **Every run's status is `partial`, never `success`** (84 of 84, ~3 known fail-closed fetch failures
  per run), so the status field carries no signal and nothing can alert on it.
- **The whole rejected corpus is re-read six times a day.** `postings_needing_extraction` selects
  `raw_payload` for every open unextracted row and Stage-A filters in Python afterward. Measured
  08-22: **6,685 rows inside the age floor, of which zero pass Stage A** — re-fetched and re-rejected
  every run, forever.

**Resolved from 1.1's ledger:** the **digest `new`-window hole**, which 1.1 recorded as "not observed
in production" and which has since been observed in production. Fixed by item 3 (D-108). Two
residuals stay open and belong in this update's own ledger at close:

- ~~**A quarantined posting is still lost permanently**~~ — **fixed 2026-08-06 by item 5 (D-110).**
  Re-measured that morning with D-108 *and* D-109 live: 2,703 open in-scope relevant pairs inside
  the age floor decompose as 1,999 mailed · **564 quarantined** · 132 never-sent recipients · 2 not
  yet due · **6 unexplained** (all pre-D-108 July residue). Quarantine was the entire live loss.
  Re-checking 23 of those URLs by hand found **16 × 200 · 4 × 403 · 2 × 404** — three distinct
  facts that `verify_apply_url` was collapsing into one `False`. The 403s (Coinbase, Akuna, Tower
  Research) were the class nobody had spotted: a WAF refusing HEAD *and* GET, with our User-Agent
  and with none, which no retry or throttle would ever have fixed. Only a definitive 404/410
  quarantines now, and verification is job-scoped (2,727 requests → 462). **The 564 already-stuck
  pairs stay lost by decision** — see the third bullet. Original diagnosis, kept for the record:
  - D-108 is holding: of 320 relevant (posting, recipient) pairs first seen in the last 5 days, 291
    mailed and **zero unexplained**.
  - Across all ages: 2,909 mailed · **784 quarantined** · 197 to never-sent recipients · 19
    unexplained (all pre-D-108 residue). About **one in five** matched roles never arrives.
  - Today's send quarantined 125 pairs behind 113 distinct URLs, 90 of them `*.greenhouse.io`.
    Twelve re-checked by hand, 1.5s apart: **11 × 200, 1 × 404**. Only the 404 was a real dead link.
  - Worked case (`haydenham10` / trading, 16 on the dashboard vs 4 in the digest): 3 shipped · 3
    quarantined that morning · **4 quarantined on 08-03 and permanently lost** · 6 correctly omitted
    (already mailed 08-03; back at the top of the table only because the board re-dated them).

  Cause: `build_digest` runs per recipient and re-verifies every URL with a fresh client, no dedupe,
  no delay, no retry — ~685 HEADs at two hosts inside a six-minute job — and `verify_apply_url`
  returns a bare bool, so 404, 429, 503 and a timeout are one answer. Fail-closed is correct (D-008);
  recording "we could not tell" as "it is dead" is not. The status code is logged nowhere, so
  throttling is inferred from burst shape plus the clean re-checks, not observed.
- **The dashboard's newest-first ordering keys on the ATS date; the digest ships on our detection
  date.** A re-stamped role returns to the top of the table looking new when it was mailed days ago
  (6 of the 16 above). Not a data bug and not in PR 4's scope; it needs a presentation call.
- **Roles already lost stay lost — decided, not deferred.** 23 to the window bug, 659 to quarantine, no
  backfill. A role a day or two old is the one worth delivering; reconstructing weeks-old history is
  effort against the wrong end of the value curve.
